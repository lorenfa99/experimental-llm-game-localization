DREAD DELUSION — BLOCKWISE INDEPENDENT TRANSLATION STAGE v11.0

PURPOSE

Produce one independent premium Italian translation using exactly one active
profile.

All hard rules come from:

01_COMPACT_CORE_RULEBOOK.txt
glossario.txt
TASK_FILE_ADDENDUM.txt

Do not read any other Italian candidate.

OUTPUT POLICY

Produce exactly one user-facing TXT.

Do not produce a QA audit, report, rationale, or candidate table.

PREPARATION

Use the universal block-processing contract.

Before splitting:

1. detect and record the source schema under the core rulebook;
2. resolve the exact TARGET_TEXT field or coordinated TARGET_TEXT fields;
3. apply any matching classification and scope in TASK_FILE_ADDENDUM.txt;
4. resolve any exact start anchor and include the anchor row;
5. lock all rows outside ACTIVE_SCOPE;
6. create the manifest using physical line number as the primary row key;
7. generate the source-signal watchlist, including task parents and stage
   chains for task sources, title/author/series signals for book sources, and
   item IDs, variant tiers, repeated names, and repeated mechanics for item
   sources, and string IDs, title/body pairs, numbered sequences, runtime
   fragments, placeholders, control labels, and repeated UI formulas for
   UI/system/general-string sources.

Split the source into exact physical-line blocks.

Schema limits:

- dialogue, task, UI, system, tutorial, subtitle, sign, map, notification, and
  general-string tables: at most 10 non-empty in-scope TARGET_ROW entries and
  12,000 characters;
- book and document tables: exactly one active physical data row per block,
  with title and body kept together and no character-based splitting;
- item, inventory, and catalogue tables: at most 5 active physical item rows
  and 12,000 characters, with every selected Name/Description variant from one
  row kept together and never split.

Create the manifest, source-signal watchlist, and temporary block workspace
with deterministic tools. Confirm preparation passes before editorial work begins.

FIRST SWEEP — TRANSLATE EVERY BLOCK

Process blocks strictly in manifest order.

For each current block:

1. read the current source block;
2. read the immediately previous and next source blocks only as context;
3. reconstruct every non-empty in-scope TARGET_ROW:
   - for dialogue: speaker, addressee, literal and pragmatic meaning, voice,
     register, rhetoric, and oral rhythm;
   - for task or journal text: title or stage function, current state, completed
     action, remaining objective, player instruction, parent-task progression,
     modality, certainty, aspect, and consequence;
   - for book or document text: title-body relationship, genre, authorial or
     narrative voice, point of view, intended reader, chronology, paragraph and
     verse structure, quoted voices, rhetoric, ideology, satire, and deliberate
     orthographic characterization;
   - for item, inventory, or catalogue text: item family, variant tier, concise
     UI name, gameplay effect, limitation, stat or skill terminology, upgrade
     relationship, repeated mechanical formula, lore segment, humor, and
     name-description consistency across the whole physical row;
   - for UI, system, tutorial, subtitle, sign, map, notification, or general
     text: screen and gameplay function, label or message type, paired title/body
     context, numbered-sequence continuity, runtime-fragment grammar, direct
     player address, capitalization, placeholder placement, and diegetic voice;
   - for every schema: gameplay function, motivation, intensity, humor,
     metaphor, rituality, repetition, wordplay, and protected markers;
4. generate the Italian translation independently;
5. apply the active profile only as a tie-breaker between already faithful,
   natural, and technically valid solutions;
6. preserve every structural rule;
7. write the translated block;
8. validate the block before continuing.

Do not defer difficult lines to a later stage.

INTERNAL PROPOSAL DEPTH

Simple low-risk row:

- one carefully checked solution.

Medium-risk row:

- at least two genuinely different internal strategies.

Humor, insult, metaphor, ritual language, wordplay, deliberate repetition,
marked rhythm, ambiguity, or long syntax:

- at least three genuinely different strategies;
- include a functional recreation when literal transfer would weaken the
  effect.

Do not output rejected alternatives.

MANDATORY SECOND SWEEP — MISSING-ISSUE SEARCH

After all first-sweep blocks pass:

1. assemble the provisional complete translation;
2. build a cross-block index for:
   - repeated exact English lines;
   - repeated and related formulas;
   - glossary terms;
   - speaker and faction groups;
   - task-parent groups, stage progression, and recurring journal formulas;
   - book series, authors, bylines, titles, document formulas, and recurring
     literary or historical terminology;
   - item families, rarity and material tiers, repeated base descriptions,
     gameplay-stat formulas, upgrade wording, and recurring UI terminology;
   - UI and system labels, control names, title/body and _T/body pairs,
     numbered sequences, intro subtitles, runtime fragments, placeholders, and
     recurring confirmation, notification, tutorial, map, and status formulas;
   - tutorial formulas;
   - recurring jokes, metaphors, and rhetorical structures;
3. revisit every block in manifest order;
4. compare each source block with its provisional Italian block;
5. actively search for:
   - omissions or additions;
   - altered motivation, modality, certainty, aspect, or intensity;
   - calques;
   - weak collocations;
   - translated syntax;
   - generic wording;
   - voice drift;
   - weakened rhetoric or rhythm;
   - lost humor, metaphor, repetition, or wordplay;
   - unclear item mechanics, weak UI names, or inconsistent variant tiers;
   - verbose or inconsistent UI labels, broken title/body relationships,
     discontinuous numbered sequences, damaged placeholders, or fragments that
     no longer combine correctly at runtime;
   - inconsistencies across blocks;
6. improve any line for which a stronger justified solution exists;
7. validate every changed block again.

The second sweep must inspect all blocks.

Do not stop because the first sweep passed technical checks.

FINAL QA

Reassemble the complete candidate.

Run deterministic validation for:

- exact physical-line count, order, source encoding, and line endings;
- exact schema and all resolved TARGET_TEXT fields;
- exact active start scope and byte-identical rows before the anchor;
- physical row keys, IDs, parents when present, and sequence;
- the source pipe count and pipe positions on every corresponding row, with no
  fixed pipe count imported from another schema;
- byte-identical non-target fields and unchanged separators between selected target fields;
- columns and empty fields;
- protected markers and boundaries;
- numeric tokens and approximation;
- glossary and capitalization;
- schema-appropriate address: dialogue Voi, narrator/task Tu, source-defined
  book/document person and register, and singular Tu for item, UI, system,
  tutorial, and notification text only when directly addressing the player;
- unchanged empty and literal null item cells;
- consistent item names, tiers, mechanics, numbers, percentages, and comparative
  modifiers across coordinated variants;
- exact runtime placeholders and correct assembly of paired, sequential, and
  composable UI/system strings;
- whitespace and duplicate spaces.

Also run a final objective grammar check.

Do not begin a third broad stylistic pass.

FINAL RESPONSE

Return only:

- the complete translated TXT;
- one concise sentence confirming completion of both block sweeps and final QA.

Then stop.

================================================================================
PROFILE A — BALANCED
================================================================================

Choose the strongest overall localization.

Tie-break order:

1. semantic fidelity;
2. native Italian;
3. schema-appropriate speaker, authorial, document, item, or interface voice;
4. rhetoric, rhythm, and UI clarity;
5. concision.

Avoid blandness, unnecessary creativity, over-literary phrasing, and mechanical
literalism.

================================================================================
PROFILE B — SEMANTIC
================================================================================

Among natural solutions, preserve the finest source distinctions:

- agent and recipient;
- motivation;
- cause and purpose;
- modality and obligation;
- certainty and uncertainty;
- aspect;
- intensity;
- economic interest;
- gameplay function;
- source-supported vulgarity.

Do not preserve English syntax when Italian can express the same meaning
naturally.

================================================================================
PROFILE C — NATURAL SPOKEN ITALIAN
================================================================================

Among equally faithful solutions, prefer Italian that sounds originally
written for the source medium, including natural spoken dialogue, natural
published prose, concise native item-interface text, and natural UI/system text.

Focus on:

- idiomatic collocations;
- natural information order;
- oral rhythm;
- concise verbs;
- believable questions, offers, commands, and threats;
- removal of translation smell.

Do not paraphrase away meaning or flatten deliberate repetition.

================================================================================
PROFILE D — VOICE AND RHETORIC
================================================================================

Among equally faithful and natural solutions, preserve:

- faction, authorial, document, item, and diegetic interface register;
- individual personality, narrative persona, item-family tone, or system-text function;
- formality and contempt;
- ritual language;
- humor and insults;
- hesitation;
- metaphor;
- repetition;
- wordplay;
- marked rhythm.

Do not invent characterization or unsupported vulgarity.

================================================================================
PROFILE E — CONSERVATIVE AND TECHNICAL
================================================================================

Among equally strong solutions, prefer the lowest-risk defensible wording.

Focus on:

- no additions or omissions;
- glossary and capitalization;
- marker boundaries;
- numbers and approximation;
- antecedents;
- schema-appropriate Tu/Voi;
- masculine-singular agreement;
- recurring terminology;
- exact item mechanics, tier relationships, empty cells, and literal null
  sentinels;
- exact UI placeholders, paired-string relationships, sequence order, and
  runtime-fragment compatibility.

Do not become stiff or mechanically literal.
